The Bookkeeper was a real prediction program. It studied real past draws, ran four methods on them, and reported its record as it stood, including the stretches where it lost.

Every draw was independent chance, so no method can beat 10% per digit in the long run. Any streak you saw, in the Bookkeeper or in yourself, was luck. Nothing you typed was saved or sent.
