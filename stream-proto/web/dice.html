Dice setup

Using dice_reader.py? Then the die is set up in its own window: here, only pick the AVATAR CAMERA and SAVE.

1. Point the dice camera straight down at the throwing square.
2. Drag a box on the picture just inside your tape.
3. Take the die out and press EMPTY TRAY. Do this again if the light or the camera moves.
4. Throw. Green = what changed (should be just the die), the box = the top face, red = the dots it counts.
5. SAVE.
