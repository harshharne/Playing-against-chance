# Playing Against Chance: stage-one storyline (v2, one die)

One hardcoded life, the same sequence of moments for every player. Chance decides how each one goes.

## Rules

**Two sources of chance**
- **The die:** one physical d6, rolled by the player. Used only for choices.
- **random.org:** never seen by the player. Runs defaults, flukes, and what happens after a miss.

**Three kinds of moment**
- **Default:** happens to you. random.org rolls a d6 on the moment's table. The player watches.
- **Choice:** the player picks an option. Each option shows its target ("4 or more", "a 6", "exactly 3"). The player rolls the die.
- **Fluke:** something small and ordinary. random.org rolls a d6. Faces 1–5: nothing. Face 6: it leaves a **mark** that changes a later moment.

**Targets on one die**, easiest to hardest: 2 or more · 3 or more · 4 or more · 5 or more · a 6 / exactly one number.
Options opened by a mark always need **one exact number**, the same face every time that mark appears (NAME = 1, RADIO = 3, PLATFORM = 4).

**Modifiers** from marks and earlier outcomes move a target one step easier or harder. The player sees the adjusted target with no explanation. A target never goes above 6 or below 2. Exact-number options are never modified.

**On a miss** random.org rolls a d6: on 1–4 you get the next easier option; on 5–6 you are **allotted** one entry from that choice's allotment list. Missing the easiest option always means allotment.

**Never shown:** percentages, odds, forecasts as numbers. Marks stay hidden until the ending.

**Style:** paint only for now. One painterly look for the avatar and the world. Each world scene is built from the outcome + place + year + age.

## Crust (0–18)

**6. Age 7 · The radio** (Fluke). A cousin leaves a broken radio at your house.
6: you take it apart and put it back together. *Mark RADIO.*

**7. Age 11 · The family** (Default)
1. A parent loses their job. *Science and Medical one harder*
2. The family moves cities. *Every option at 16 one harder*
3. A sibling is born; you mind the baby after school. *Medical one harder*
4. A grandfather moves in and reads to you every night. *Arts one easier*
5. An uncle's shop does well; he pays for tuition. *Science and Medical one easier*
6. Nothing happens.

**8. Age 16 · The stream** (Choice)

| Commerce | 4 or more |

Modifiers: POOR makes Science one harder · RICH makes Medical one easier.

## Adulthood (18–60)

**9. Age 19 · The train** (Fluke). You miss a train by ten seconds.
6: on the platform, you get talking to a stranger. *Mark PLATFORM.*

**10. Age 23 · The economy** (Default). The year you finish studying:
1–2. a recession *(every option at 26 one harder)*
3–4. an ordinary year
5–6. your field is suddenly the one everyone wants *(every option at 26 one easier)*

**11. Age 26 · The offer** (Choice)

| Option | Target |
|---|---|

| A friend's startup | 3 or more |
| Any job that pays this month | 2 or more |
| *NAME only:* the interview meant for someone with your name, spelled right | exactly 1 |

**12. Age 31 · Marriage** (Choice)

| Option | Target |
|---|---|
| Someone you choose (the family has to agree) | 5 or more |
| The family's choice | 3 or more |
| No one, for now (the aunts have opinions) | 2 or more |
| *PLATFORM only:* the stranger from the platform | exactly 4 |

Allotted: the family's choice, anyway · a wedding called off a week before.

**13. Age 38 · The typo** (Fluke). You send an email to the whole office with a typo in it.
6: everyone laughs. Someone writes it on the whiteboard. *Mark TYPO.*

**14. Age 45 · The office** (Default)
1–2. Restructured out. *TYPO: the typo became the product's name; you're kept on.*
3–4. Nothing changes.
5. Promoted.
6. Your boss leaves suddenly; the desk is yours.

**15. Age 47 · The buried layer** (Default)
- HIDDEN: what the body hid comes up. A condition you were born with surfaces.
- Otherwise: 1. a scare that turns out to be nothing · 2–5. nothing, yet · 6. a clerical error declares you dead. *NAME: always this, since the dead man is the one with your name spelled right.*
